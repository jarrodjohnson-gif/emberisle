# Audio credits

- `amb_sea.mp3`: original procedural synthesis by Emberisle contributors. CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. No recording or third-party sample is used. The reproducible recipe is `scripts/make-ambience.mjs`: low-passed brown noise, a 0.1 Hz swell, a 20-second loop, a two-second tail crossfade and a natural zero-crossing loop point without an edge fade. The generator masters this candidate once through the shared levelling tool, using the separate ambience target of −30.6 LUFS.
