# Offsuit × Catan

The look and feel of the browser table, as a small design system. Jarrod, 2026-10-04: "I want ux to be
simple but polished like wow this is so simple but elegant catan like and polished. AAA game style but
simple and just works", and "'Offsuit' ios poker game is an extremely simple version. I'd like a mix of
that and 'catan'." Issue #434.

**North star: Offsuit × Catan.** Offsuit's restraint around Catan's board. The island is warm, readable and
tactile; everything around it is almost nothing: few elements, big confident type, flat clean surfaces, one
thing to do at a time. Where the current UI adds chrome, text or panels, the default is to remove.

This builds on [ux-review-2026-10.md](ux-review-2026-10.md) (its findings and its "North star" still hold)
and on the build bible's §3.4 (feel), §4 (in-game screen), §7 (sounds) and §8 (animation). It does not
re-file anything in #416-#425, #430, #129, #135 or #323; it says how they should *look* when they land.

Evidence: a pass on `main` at `d967ad2`, title → practice → roll-off → first placement → first roll → main,
at 1280x720, 390x844 (touch) and 844x390 (touch). Frame names below are the ux-review's (`desk-05-main`
etc., regenerated by `node scripts/shots.mjs`). Offsuit references: its App Store screenshots (poker and
blackjack).

|---|---|---|

Offsuit shows a wordmark and one button. |
| Roll-off (`desk-02-rolloff`) | A wordmark pill, How to play, Leave, four seat cards each saying "0 goods · 0 fortunes", a long instruction sentence, **five white "0" cards**, a black Roll bar and a log line. | The hand is the brightest block on screen and holds nothing. The instruction is two sentences. |

| First roll (`desk-05-main`) | Two 36 px dice bottom-left with a grey 14 px "9"; the same roll in the roll line and the log line. | The one exciting number on the table is the smallest text on it, shown three times. |

What already works and should be kept: the island itself (warm caps, rimmed number tokens from
[tokens.md](tokens.md), sheep, boats, the wayfarer), the blurred ocean behind the title card, the trade
toast, the +N/−N flash on the hand, the pip dice faces.

## What to borrow

### From Offsuit

- **One hero number per screen.** Offsuit's chip count (`11,250`) and win odds (`71%`) are set huge, thin
  and alone. Ours: the roll sum when it lands, your points, the counts on your hand. Nothing else gets big.
- **Very few elements.** A screen has a header number, one object (the cards), two or three action tiles.
  Ours: the island, your hand, the one next action.
- **Flat, rounded, quiet surfaces.** Dark tiles with ~20 px corners, no borders, no shadows, no gradients
  on controls; the colour is in the content (the cards), never in the chrome.
- **Actions as big soft tiles, worded as the action with its amount** ("Call 10", "Raise 20"). Ours:
  "Path · 1 timber 1 clay" is already this shape; make it the button, not a tooltip.
- **One thing at a time.** Stats appear as a card *over* the table when asked, then leave. Nothing sits
  on screen "just in case".
- **Snappy, tactile motion.** Cards flip and slide with short eased motion; the press is felt.

### From Catan

- **The board is the hero.** Warm, saturated, readable from across the table; the HUD is a rim.
- **Strong resource identity.** Every resource has one colour and one icon everywhere it appears: the
  hex, the hand card, the cost on a button, the trade picker, the gains flash. Ours today: colour on the
  board, white cards in the hand.
- **Number tokens with probability you can see.** Done (#134, [tokens.md](tokens.md)). Keep the red 6 and 8.
- **Tactile pieces.** Outposts, paths and strongholds that land with weight and a woody knock.
- **Warm table tone.** Cream and stone, not sterile white or black.

## Principles

1. **Board first.** The island takes the screen; the HUD is a thin rim and never covers the middle.
2. **One thing at a time.** Show only what the player can do now; hide what is irrelevant this phase
   (illegal = hidden, bible §4.2).
3. **One primary per screen.** Exactly one filled primary button on any screen; everything else is quiet.
4. **Numbers are the heroes, words are the last resort.** Counts, points and the roll are big; sentences
   are short, one voice, one place.
5. **Colour is information.** Colour belongs to resources and seats; chrome is neutral stone, ink and glass.
6. **Flat, warm, quiet surfaces.** One glass, one solid surface, no borders where spacing will do.
7. **Every action is felt.** Every press clicks and scales, every piece lands and knocks, every turn
   change moves something, all under 320 ms.
8. **Hide complexity until asked.** Rules, costs, fortunes and logs live one tap away, not on screen.

## Design system

These are the values. #435 puts them in `src/styles.css` as Tailwind v4 theme variables and applies them to
the shared primitive (`src/components/ui/button.tsx`); the issues below adopt them screen by screen.

### Type

One UI family (the system sans already in `styles.css`: `ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe
UI"`), one display family (`--font-display`, Iowan Old Style) used only for the wordmark and the win line.
Five sizes; nothing else.

| Token | Size / line | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `text-caption` | 12 / 16 px | 500 | Meta only: turn number, a count's unit, the log line. Sentence case. |
| `text-body` | 15 / 22 px | 500 | The phase sentence, buttons, form fields, toasts. |
| `text-title` | 20 / 26 px, −0.01em | 600 | Sheet titles (Trade, Discard), a seat's points. |
| `text-number` | 28 / 32 px | 600, tabular | Hand counts, the lobby code, your points. |
| `text-display` | 56 / 60 px, −0.02em | 600 | The roll sum while it lands, the wordmark, the win line. |

- No uppercase-with-tracking labels (`A LIVING ISLAND`, `YOUR NAME`, `TIMBER`, the win-table headers):
  they read as admin UI. Sentence case at `text-caption`, or no label.
- All counts `tabular-nums` so they do not jitter when they change.
- Minimum on a phone: 12 px, and only for meta.

### Spacing

Tailwind's 4 px base, used on these steps only: **4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48**.

- Screen gutter: 16 px on phones, 24 px from 1024 px wide.
- Between HUD groups (seat rail, hand, actions): 12 px. Inside a group: 8 px. Inside a chip: 12 px x, 8 px y.
- Touch targets 44 px minimum (the ux-review measured these; keep it).
- When in doubt, add space and remove a border.

### Colour

Neutrals (existing tokens, unchanged, plus `glass`, which is new and lands with #424):

| Token | Value | Use | Contrast |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bg` (stone) | `#efeae0` | page background | — |
| `surface` | `#f7f4ee` | opaque sheets (trade, discard, how to play) | — |
| `raised` | `#ffffff` | fields, the selected tile | — |
| `fg` (ink) | `#1c1915` | text, the primary button fill | 16:1 on surface |
| `muted` | `#6b645a` | caption text | 5.3:1 on surface, 5.8 on white |
| `border` | `#d8d1c3` | fields only | non-text |
| `sea` / `sea-ink` | `#2a8f8a` / `#1b726e` | links, focus ring, Tide | ink 5.7:1 with white |
| `accent` / `accent-ink` | `#c45c3e` / `#a94b30` | Ember; a 7 | ink 5.6:1 with white |
| `danger` | `#b3261e` | errors, can't-afford (new, #435) | 6.5:1 on white |
| `glass` | white ≥ 70 % + 16 px blur (`--color-glass`, **new in #424**, not on main yet) | every text chip over the island | text ≥ 4.5:1 sampled over the board |

Resources, new. The fill is the terrain cap's own base colour from `PAINT`
(`src/lib/scene/palette.ts`), so the hand card *is* the hex. Text on a fill uses its `-on` colour:

| Resource | Fill token | Value | `-on` | Contrast of `-on` on fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Timber | `timber` | `#2f6b3a` | white | 6.4:1 |
| Clay | `clay` | `#b5522a` | white | 5.0:1 |
| Wool | `wool` | `#8fbf5a` | ink `#1c1915` | 8.1:1 |
| Grain | `grain` | `#e0b13a` | ink `#1c1915` | 8.8:1 |
| Ore | `ore` | `#6e7580` | white | 4.7:1 |

Targets: text on any fill **≥ 4.5:1** (WCAG 1.4.3); a resource fill against the glass or stone around it
**≥ 3:1 or a 1 px inner ring** of `black/10` (wool and grain are 1.8 and 1.7 on stone, so they get the
ring). Never rely on hue alone: every resource also has its icon (lucide, as today), and colour-blind seat
work stays with #312.

Seats keep `PLAYER_COLORS` (#142). Seat colour appears in exactly three places: the seat dot, your pieces,
and your legal-spot pulse.

### Radii

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `rounded-control` | 12 px (Jarrod 2026-10-04; supersedes the bible §3.4's 8 px) | buttons, fields, dice, swatches' focus ring |
| `rounded-chip` | 16 px | text chips, hand cards, seat cards, toasts |
| `rounded-sheet` | 24 px | sheets and modals (trade, discard, how to play, win, title card) |
| `rounded-full` | pill | swatches, avatars, the turn dot |

Nested corners: inner radius = outer radius − padding. So a sheet (24) pads **12 px** around a control
(12) or a chip-sized card (it then shows 12, not 16: inside a sheet, cards use `rounded-control`); a chip
(16) pads **4 px** around a control (12). Today the code uses 4, 8, 12, 16, 20
and 28 px; the 4 / 20 / 28 cases fold into the table above as each screen is touched.

### Surfaces

Two, and only two:

- **Glass** for anything that sits over the island and carries text: `--color-glass` (new in #424, white ≥ 70 %),
  `backdrop-blur` 16 px, no border, `rounded-chip`. The log line moves onto it.
- **Solid** `surface` for sheets that take the centre (trade, discard, how to play, win), `rounded-sheet`,
  with one soft shadow (`0 8px 32px rgb(28 25 21 / 0.18)`) and no border.

No third treatment: `bg-white/45` and `bg-raised` text chips go. No borders on chips (spacing and the glass
edge are enough). No gradients on controls.

### Buttons

| Level | Look | Size | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | `fg` fill, `bg` text, no border | 48 px tall, full width on phones | The one next action: Roll, Play, Start, Confirm discard, Send offer. **One per screen.** |
| **Secondary** | `raised` fill, `fg` text, 1 px inner ring `black/10` (edge contrast on `surface` sheets, WCAG 1.4.11), `rounded-control` | 44 px | Real alternatives: Trade, Join, Host, a build you can afford. |
| **Quiet** | text only, `fg`, hover `fg/5` (this overrides the bible §3.4's stone-filled button for this level) | 44 px | Back, Cancel, How to play, Leave, End turn when there is still something to do. |
| **Disabled build** | secondary at 50 % with its price (`Path · 1 timber 1 clay`) | 44 px | Only the three builds in `main` (bible §4.2). |

- Colour buttons (`sea`, `accent` variants) are retired as screens are touched: colour is information,
  not hierarchy. End turn becomes Primary once nothing else is affordable, Quiet before; as Quiet it stays
  on screen at its full 44 px target, never hidden or shrunk.
- Press: scale 0.97, 80 ms, `--ease-out`, `ui_click` on pointerdown (#425, PR #431).
- Focus: 2 px `sea-ink` outline, 2 px offset, on `:focus-visible` only.
- Labels are verbs plus the amount where there is one: "Roll", "Place outpost", "Bank 4 ore → 1 grain".

### Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--duration-press` | 80 ms | button press scale |
| `--duration-quick` | 150 ms | hover, toggles, a die face settling |
| `--duration-base` | 220 ms | chips and toasts entering, a path growing, the hand appearing |
| `--duration-settle` | 280 ms | an outpost landing, a sheet opening (a stronghold adds its 1.05 bounce: 320 ms, bible §8) |
| `--duration-pulse` | 1.2 s, sine, loop | the legal-spot pulse (#437), the only looping motion |
| `--duration-moment` | 900 ms | how long the roll holds centre screen |
| `--ease-out` | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` | everything that enters or moves |
| `--ease-snap` | `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` | things that land: pieces, a count bumping, the dice |
| `--ease-in` | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 1, 1)` | things that leave (exit at about 0.7 × the entry time) |

- Move transform and opacity only; never animate layout.
- Nothing over 320 ms except the roll moment, the legal-spot pulse and the wayfarer's walk (bible §8: 600-900 ms).
- No camera shake, no confetti, no looping UI animation except the legal-spot pulse.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: CSS durations collapse to 1 ms. That does not reach three.js: under
  reduced motion the legal-spot pulse and any other loop **stop and hold a steady state** (a code switch in
  the renderer, not a duration), as #382 did and #437 specifies. Sounds still play.

### Sound pairing

Sound lands on **contact**, not on intent: a button clicks on pointerdown, but a piece knocks when its drop
ends, dice land when they freeze, a gain ticks when the hand count changes.

| Motion | Sound (bible §7) | Fires at |
|---|---|---|
| Button press (80 ms) | `ui_click` | pointerdown |
| Esc / Back / Cancel | `ui_back` | keydown / pointerdown |
| Ready, Start, trade Yes | `ui_confirm` | on the confirmed state, not the press |
| Illegal pick, bad code | `ui_error` | on the error |
| Path grows (220 ms) | `path_place` | end of the grow |
| Outpost drops (`--duration-settle`, 280 ms) | `outpost_place` | the landing frame |
| Stronghold settles (320 ms) | `stronghold_place` | the landing frame |
| Dice freeze | `dice_land` | the freeze |
| Hand count rises | `chip_gain` | once per roll, not per hex |
| Win line | `win` | when it appears |

Mix stays the bible's: UI −6 dB under pieces, ambience −18 dB, no music.

## The board look target

The island should read like a lit Catan board on a table, seen by someone leaning over it.

- **Warm, low-saturation light** with soft shadows; caps saturated, the sea a calm teal that never
  competes with the land (#135 owns lights and the camera).
- **Tokens and pieces read first,** props second: the clear ring around every token
  ([tokens.md](tokens.md)), two-step piece bases ([pieces.md](pieces.md), #238).
- **Legal spots pulse in your colour** (#437), not cream for everyone.
- **Pieces land** with a short drop and a knock (#438).
- **Paying hexes glow once** after a roll so the player sees why they got goods (#441).
- **The title island** keeps its slow orbit with the card over the blurred sea; in play the overhead
  camera fits the hole the HUD leaves (#129, #135, #422).
- Nothing on the board is UI: no labels floating over hexes, no outlines except the legal pulse.
