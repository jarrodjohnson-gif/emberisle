# Pieces Jelly — the build prompt (#638)

Written 2026-10-16 after the owner looked at the first pass ("don't love the house, castle is cool, kinda watchtower look,
what jelly prompt did you use?"). The first pass had no prompt; this is it. Same look as `hex-jelly-prompt.md` and
`harbor-jelly-prompt.md`: chunky, rounded, glossy candy forms, soft light, warm creases, toy-town proportions.

PROMPT

Build the players' pieces for a jelly hex island: the paths, outposts and strongholds each seat builds. They are toys
placed on a model, not part of the scenery: bolder, simpler and more saturated than anything on the hexes, so a glance at
the board shows who has built where. Every piece is a little sculpture in its seat's colour, glossy like a boiled sweet,
standing on a dark ring with a cream band (the board's mark for "a player's piece"), and each kind has its own silhouette
so it reads by shape as well as colour, from the overhead phone view and the tilted diorama.

THE GAME (what must stay true)
- A path spans one hex edge; an outpost or stronghold stands on one corner (a 0.14 disc kept flat for it).
- Seats are told apart by colour only (red, blue, cream, purple today), so the seat colour dominates what the camera
  sees: the top of each piece. The cream seat must still read on the pale hexes (the dark ring does that).
- Outpost and stronghold must be tellable apart at a glance, and from a path, even in greyscale: by height and outline.
- Each lands with a small jelly squash (and later dust, #642); nothing on a piece moves at rest.

THE PATH
- A causeway along the edge: a rounded bar in the seat colour, pillowy like a jelly sweet, on cream kerbs over a dark rim,
  its ends rounded. Wide enough to read as a line of colour round the hexes.

THE OUTPOST
- Today's house shape, made jelly (the owner's pick, 2026-10-16, after a round hut and then an A-frame and a tower-house
  were tried and turned down): a chunky block **all in the seat's colour** under a steep gable roof a shade deeper, the
  eaves overhanging; soft bevels everywhere, glossy.
- A dark door and a warm lit window facing the camera, a cream chimney.
- About 0.27 across and 0.3 tall (before the 1.15 board scale): lower and squarer than the keep, a pitched roof against
  its round crenellated top.

THE STRONGHOLD (keep, refine)
- The owner liked it: a **watchtower keep**. A round tower of the seat colour, taller than an outpost (about 0.42 with
  the banner), a deeper band and crenels round its top, a little side turret with a cone roof, arrow slits and a door.
- Refine: rounder, softer crenels (pillowy, not boxes), a slight taper. (A pennant that flutters in the landing is left for #642.)

LOOK
- One glossy material for all pieces (clearcoat), vertex colours; the seat colour lightly lighter on walls, deeper on
  roofs and tops; doors dark, windows warm. Chunky, toy-like, nothing spindly.

PERFORMANCE
- Each piece one merged mesh (one draw), shared material, built when it lands; on the first frame, so small code.
